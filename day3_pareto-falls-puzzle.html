🧩 The Falls Pareto Puzzle

One month of falls-by-location data. Find the "vital few" locations that make up roughly 80% of all falls — and unlock the code.

1 The Puzzle

Teams are given one month of falls-by-location data and must complete a Pareto analysis: calculate each location's share of total falls, order from largest to smallest, and identify which locations make up roughly 80% of all falls (the "vital few").
